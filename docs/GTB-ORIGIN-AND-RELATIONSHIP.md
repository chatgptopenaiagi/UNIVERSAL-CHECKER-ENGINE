## The conceptual source

The owner identifies **GITHUB TEST BUTTON (GTB-001)** as the inspiration for UCE. The originating project is [chatgptopenaiagi/GITHUB-TEST-BUTTON](https://github.com/chatgptopenaiagi/GITHUB-TEST-BUTTON).

The interaction being generalized is:

**ONE BUTTON → MULTIPLE TESTS → STRUCTURED RESULTS → HUMAN-READABLE EXPLANATIONS**

UCE asks whether that pattern could help people understand many other technologies. It preserves the interaction and its emphasis on distinct findings while proposing broader research into common diagnostic conventions.

## Relationship and separation

| GTB's role in this concept | UCE's proposed extension |
|---|---|
| A GitHub-focused diagnostic interaction | A possible family spanning multiple domains |
| Distinct questions about connection, identity, access, and operations | A common vocabulary that preserves these distinctions across suitable domains |
| Results that people can inspect and understand | Shared principles for evidence and human-readable reporting |
| A separate project with its own implementation and evidence | A documentation-only foundation with no inherited proof of functionality |

UCE is not a modification, reorganization, replacement, or copied distribution of GTB. This mission does not change GTB files, branches, settings, issues, or releases. Referencing the concept does not establish code reuse, shared dependencies, compatibility, or endorsement by any third party.

Future experiments might learn from GTB's documented decisions, but would need separate authorization and their own evidence. See [original concept](ORIGINAL-CONCEPT.md), [diagnostic contract](UNIVERSAL-DIAGNOSTIC-CONTRACT.md), and [roadmap](FUTURE-ROADMAP.md).
